# Power BI Build Guide

This repository does not include a proprietary `.pbix` file. Use the CSV files or the MySQL database as the Power BI source.

## Recommended Pages

### Page 1 — Executive Overview
Cards:
- Revenue
- Orders
- AOV
- Gross Profit
- Gross Margin %
- Repeat Customer Rate

Visuals:
- Monthly Revenue
- Revenue by Category
- Revenue by Acquisition Channel
- Top 10 Products

### Page 2 — Marketing Performance
Cards:
- Spend
- Impressions
- Clicks
- CTR
- Conversions
- Cost per Conversion
- Estimated ROAS

Visuals:
- Spend vs Estimated Revenue by Channel
- CTR by Channel
- Conversion Rate by Channel
- Campaign Performance Table

### Page 3 — Customer Analytics
- RFM Segment distribution
- Revenue by segment
- Customers by segment
- Repeat rate by acquisition channel
- At-risk customer table

### Page 4 — Website Funnel
- Sessions
- Add to Cart
- Checkout
- Purchases
- Funnel conversion
- Device conversion comparison
- Channel conversion comparison

## Suggested Slicers
- Date
- City
- Acquisition Channel
- Product Category
- Device
- Campaign Channel

## Design Principle
Keep the dashboard decision-oriented:
**KPI → Trend → Driver → Insight → Action**
